# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in Quiverstone projects and
communities a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information — including AWS account details — without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all Quiverstone project spaces — repositories, issues, discussions,
and community forums — and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers through
[quiverstone.io/contact](https://www.quiverstone.io/contact/). All reports will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do
not align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behavior they deem
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
